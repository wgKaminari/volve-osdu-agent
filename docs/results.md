## Read this first (caveats)

- **No held-out set.** The rules regexes and curve dictionary were written after reading these same 13 bundles, so its scores are optimistic (fit to the test set). The agent's prompts are generic and were not tuned per wellbore.
- **Curve labels are not independent of the rules dictionary**: both were drafted by the same author with the same unit conventions, so rules curve-unit accuracy is near 100% by construction. The LLM systems' curve numbers, after owner review, are the real signal.
- **Small n.** 13 wellbores, header gold for 11; some per-field rows have 1-7 values. The confidence intervals are wide on purpose.
- **Disputed gold is excluded from accuracy** and listed below with each system's value; gold is never edited to match a system.
- **Local small model.** LLM numbers come from the model and settings in Run configuration; a stronger hosted model only needs a different `.env`.

## Changes made after looking at results (all reported)

- **Agent run 1** ([results_agent_run1.md](results_agent_run1.md)) showed facts that were quoted verbatim but were the wrong kind of fact or the wrong wellbore (TD 347 m taken as KB elevation; the kick-off point of 15/9-F-11 T2 given to 15/9-F-11). Rule 8 gained generic semantic checks: fact keyword in the quote, plausible range, wellbore attribution. Prompts were not changed. Compare the two reports for the effect.
- **Agent identity fallback:** when the model abstains on the wellbore name, the most specific header name is used and flagged (`identity_fallback`) instead of building nothing.
- **llm_only parser:** made lenient (keep valid items, drop malformed ones), so the baseline is judged on its answers, not on a strict parser that discarded whole replies.
